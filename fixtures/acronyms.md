## Why MQTT matters

The heading above names MQTT; the body is where it should be explained, and
MQTT is not explained here either.

See [the specification](https://example.com/ISO15118) for details, and run
`OCPI` locally. The note is in a footnote.[^ft]

The UK and the EU use AC and DC chargers, and CitrineOS runs on ISO-15118 v2.0.

[^ft]: OCPI 2.2.1, the Open Charge Point Interface.
