A heat pump has three parts: a compressor, a coil and a valve. The coil is called the evaporator, and it pulls heat from outside air. In other words, the pump moves heat rather than making it.

Our team has worked on heat pumps for a long time. We have talked to many people about them. They all had views on the topic. The meetings were long and full of ideas. We wrote notes after each one. The notes went into a shared folder. The folder is now quite large. We plan to tidy it next quarter with the whole team.
